How it works

You read in the reader you already use. ReadTrans sits beside it and translates.

1Open your reader

Open a book in a reader such as Kindle or Apple Books.

2Pick it in ReadTrans

Choose that reader window in ReadTrans. The first time only, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility.

3Turn the page

Turn pages from ReadTrans. The reader turns with it and the translation beside it changes right away.

Works with

ReadTrans translates the page on your screen, so it never opens or changes your eBook files.

Frequently asked questions

Does my book leave my Mac?

Not with the default translation. Reading the text on screen and translating it both happen on your Mac, and screen images are not saved. What goes online is the license check (key and device name) and the book title, looked up so the translation fits the book. Only if you turn on AI API translation is the text of the current page sent to the AI provider you chose.

How much does AI API translation cost?

The AI provider bills your own account, not ReadTrans, and prices vary widely between providers. The app settings show an estimated cost per provider and your running estimate. You can also use only the free on-device translation.

Which readers does it work with?

Tested so far: Kindle and Apple Books on Mac. PDF and image files (PDF·PNG·JPEG·TIFF·HEIC) open directly in ReadTrans without a reader.

When is the Windows version coming?

It is in progress. We will post the date here once it is set. A license bought for Mac is planned to work on Windows too.

Can I get a refund?

Ask at support@ezlong.com within 14 days of purchase and we refund, no questions asked. Fees paid to AI providers cannot be refunded by ReadTrans.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

One license activates up to two devices. To move it, choose “Deactivate on this Mac” in the app settings and activate on the new device.
