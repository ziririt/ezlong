Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 (finalized on launch)

ReadTrans (the "app") is built to keep your books and personal data on your Mac as much as possible. This page explains what the app and this website send, and where.

1. What stays on your Mac

  • E-book screens and their text: the free built-in translator recognizes and translates text entirely on your Mac. Page images are not stored.
  • Translations, bookmarks, notes, font settings and window size are stored only on your Mac.
  • AI provider API keys are stored only in your Mac's Keychain.

2. What the app sends over the internet

  • License checks: your license key and device name are sent to the license server of our reseller, Lemon Squeezy, when you activate and periodically afterwards. No book content is sent.
  • AI API translation (optional): only if a licensed user adds an API key and turns it on, the text of the current page (text, not a screenshot) and translation instructions are sent with the user's key to the AI provider the user chose. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply. You can turn this off or pause it at any time.
  • Model download: the app may download the translation model on first launch. No information identifying you is sent.
  • Book information: to fit translations to the book, the title of the book you are reading is sent to the public catalogue service Open Library (openlibrary.org) to look up its author, subject and year. If a licensed user has AI API translation on, the title and those facts are also sent once to the AI provider they chose. No book text is sent, and the resulting book notes are stored encrypted only on your Mac.

3. Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. ReadTrans never receives your card details. We may see your email address and order history through Lemon Squeezy to confirm purchases and provide support.

4. Website

This website does not track you with cookies or use advertising trackers. It uses the fonts already on your device, and the EZLONG apps bar at the top is loaded from ezlong.com.

5. Diagnostic log

The app keeps a diagnostic log on your Mac containing only times, event names and durations, never book text. It is not sent automatically; we only see it if you send it to us when asking for help.
