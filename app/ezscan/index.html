ezScan

One-shot document scanner for iPhone and Android

Point, shoot, done. ezScan finds the page, removes the background, flattens curled book pages and bent receipts, and cleans up the image so it looks like it came from a flatbed scanner. No manual corner dragging.

  • Books: two-page spreads are split into separate pages and the curve near the spine is flattened.
  • Receipts and documents: bent paper is straightened and the background is removed.
  • Color, grayscale and black-and-white cleanup.
  • Save to Photos or share as PDF.
  • Everything is processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
