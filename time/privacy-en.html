This policy explains what information the mobile application Long Time, Easy Life (the "App") collects, why it collects it, and the choices you have. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer trading as ezlong.com (the "Developer", "we", "us"), who acts as the data controller.

We built this App to be a calm bedside clock. It asks for as little as it can, and it does not require an account.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location

If you grant location permission, the App uses your device's approximate coordinates (latitude and longitude) to show the weather where you are, and to select background photos that match your season and local conditions. Coordinates are sent to our weather service to retrieve a forecast and to a reverse-geocoding service to convert them into a place name.

We do not store your coordinates in a form that identifies you. Our weather service caches forecast results by rounded location so that repeated requests for the same area do not trigger a new lookup; this cache is not linked to any user identifier.

You can use the App without granting location permission. Weather features will be limited or fall back to a default city.

1.2 Advertising Identifiers

The App displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Ad networks may use device advertising identifiers and similar technologies to select and measure ads.

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before any tracking that uses the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see the same number of ads — they will simply be less relevant to you. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

If you purchase the ad-free upgrade, advertising is removed and the ad SDK stops requesting ads.

1.3 Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a device registration token so that we can deliver occasional messages about the App. This token identifies a device installation, not a person, and is deleted when you uninstall the App. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

1.4 Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only the transaction status needed to unlock the features you bought.

1.5 Diagnostic and Usage Data

We may receive aggregated, non-identifying information such as device model, operating system version, crash reports, and feature usage counts, in order to find and fix problems. This data is not used to identify you.

1.6 What We Do Not Collect

The App has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone input, or health data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To show weather and choose background photos for your location, season, and time of day
  • To deliver and measure advertising, unless you have purchased the ad-free upgrade
  • To send notifications you have opted into
  • To keep the service reliable and to diagnose errors
  • To improve features based on how the App is actually used

5. Selling and Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, the use of advertising identifiers for personalised advertising may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out by declining the tracking prompt on iOS, or by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in your Android settings.

6. Retention

We do not operate a user database. Your settings — playlist filters, quote topics, background preferences, listening history — are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App. Cached weather results expire automatically. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as it is useful for fixing problems.

7. International Transfers

Our servers and the third-party services listed above operate in multiple countries, including the United States and the Republic of Korea. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, the receiving service providers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

8. Your Rights

You can withdraw location, tracking, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Withdrawing location permission will limit weather features but will not otherwise affect the App.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because the App does not maintain accounts, we usually hold no data that can be linked to you individually; if you believe we do, contact us at the address in section 11 and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

EEA and UK users also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age, such as in parts of the EEA, and under 14 in the Republic of Korea). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
