Questions

How do I paste it?

Press “Copy result”, click a single empty cell in Excel or Google Sheets and paste. The header row and every cell fall into place.

What if the table is broken?

It is rebuilt first — missing cells, a missing divider row or uneven spacing are all fixed before conversion.

Several tables in one answer?

All of them are converted, with a blank line between tables.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The conversion happens entirely inside this browser tab.
