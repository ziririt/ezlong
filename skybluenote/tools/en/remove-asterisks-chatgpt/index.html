Questions

Why are there asterisks?

ChatGPT writes in Markdown. Words wrapped in two asterisks look bold in the chat, but apps that do not read Markdown show the asterisks as plain characters.

What gets removed?

Bold and italic asterisks, # heading marks, opening filler greetings, emoji and stray asterisks at line ends. Bullets become tidy dots and broken tables are realigned.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside this browser tab, and it works offline.
