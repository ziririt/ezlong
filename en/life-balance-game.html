What is Balance Game: My Life?

Most of us never actually sit down and rank what we value most — we discover it by accident, usually in the moment we're forced to give something up. Balance Game: My Life speeds that discovery up on purpose. Across 10 scenarios, you're asked to choose between two things you genuinely care about — family time versus income, freedom versus stability, health versus achievement — with no "both" option allowed.

Your 10 choices build a profile across 8 core values: health, family, freedom, financial security, achievement, meaning, growth, and authenticity. Six questions are the same for everyone; three are tailored to your age range; the final question pits your own top two values directly against each other. The game then checks that profile against how you say you're actually spending your time right now, shows you a 0-100 alignment score, and hands you a concrete action plan — from something you can do in the next 24 hours to a 90-day target.

Why Take This?

It's easy to drift for years toward a life optimized for something you never consciously chose — more income at the cost of family time, or stability at the cost of the freedom you actually wanted. Nothing about that drift feels dramatic in the moment, which is exactly why it goes unnoticed for so long. Forcing an explicit trade-off, ten times in a row, is a fast way to surface a mismatch before it costs you another few years.

This isn't a personality label or a verdict on how you should live. It's a short, honest mirror — useful precisely because most of us never bother to look, and because it ends with something specific to actually do about what you find.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Balance Game: My Life?

It's a free self-reflection game where you make 10 forced-choice decisions between competing life values — health, family, freedom, financial security, achievement, meaning, growth, and authenticity — to discover which ones you actually prioritize, then compare that against how you're currently living.

How long does it take?

About 5 to 7 minutes for the 10 questions, the alignment check, and your results. No account creation or personal data collection is required.

Is this a clinical psychological test?

No. It's an informal self-reflection tool designed to prompt honest thinking about your priorities, not a clinical or diagnostic psychological assessment. It isn't a substitute for professional counseling.

What are the eight core values it measures?

Health, family, freedom, financial security, achievement, meaning, growth, and authenticity. Your 10 forced-choice answers are used to estimate which of these you weigh most heavily when they conflict with each other.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Balance Game: My Life is completely free and requires no sign-up, no account, and no personal information of any kind.

Does it save my results?

Yes. Your result is saved automatically in your browser, and you can also copy a short result code to reload it later on any device. The game will also prompt you for an optional 30-day check-in to see whether your actual life caught up with your stated values.
