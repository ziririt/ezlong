What is the Bull vs Bear Sentiment Scorecard?

Every trading day brings a flood of headlines, earnings reports, Fed commentary, and price swings — and it's genuinely hard to tell whether, on balance, today was a bullish day or a bearish one for US stocks. The Bull vs Bear Sentiment Scorecard solves this by having AI read the day's market-moving information and distill it into one simple number: a bullish-vs-bearish split, such as 60 vs 40.

The scorecard tracks major benchmarks and stocks including QQQ, SPY, TSLA, NVDA, and AAPL, and layers in macro context from the VIX volatility index, interest rates, and currency markets. Instead of a single opaque score, every factor behind the number is listed individually, so you can see exactly why the market leaned bullish or bearish that day.

It refreshes five times per trading day, giving you a running read on how sentiment evolves from the pre-market session through the close — without you needing to track five different news sites yourself.

Why Use This Tool?

Market narratives change fast, and it's easy to overweight the last headline you read. Having an AI systematically tally today's bullish and bearish factors — rather than relying on gut feeling or social media noise — gives you a more balanced, repeatable read on sentiment.

Because the scorecard updates five times a day, you can also see intraday sentiment shifts: for example, a market that opens bearish on weak jobs data but turns more bullish by the close on a dovish Fed comment. That kind of context is hard to get from a single end-of-day headline.
