Deep Buying Rules

The Bigger the Drop, the More It's Time to Buy

The larger the drawdown from the prior high, the larger the scaled-buy allocation, lowering the average cost basis. This only works, though, if cash has already been set aside in advance — taking profits near highs to build that cash reserve is what makes this strategy function.

Drop of 15%+ Deploy 1.5x the base buy amount
Drop of 25%+ Deploy 2.0x the base buy amount
Drop of 45%+ Deploy 3.0x the base buy amount
Downtrend (Gear 1) — a zone for holding off on swing buys

During a downtrend, holding cash and waiting is recommended. Once the uptrend is confirmed to have returned (back to Gear 3), deploying the accumulated cash into deep buys completes the cycle. Take profits near highs → build cash → deep-buy on sharp drops — that's the full strategy.

How is the Swing Signal score calculated?

Buy attractiveness (0-100pt) = Σ [ factorᵢ × weightᵢ ]  (i = 1 to 8)

Eight independent factors are calculated in parallel, then combined by weight — Gear status (0-25) + RSI zone (0-20) + RSI momentum adjustment (±5) + short-term trend alignment (0-15) + market environment/VIX (0-15) + composite MACD (0-14) + proximity to the 52-week low (0-5) + price pattern HL/LH (±3).

Each factor isn't just a raw indicator value — it's adjusted non-linearly based on the current market regime. RSI isn't used as a single value; it's combined with a 5-day sliding window to separately identify an exit from oversold or an entry into overbought (±5pt), and the price pattern compares the last 5 days' highs/lows against the prior 20-day range to compute a Higher Low (+3pt) / Lower High (-3pt). MACD combines the line's sign (medium-term trend direction) + the histogram's absolute value (short-term acceleration) + the directional change versus 5 days earlier, compressing all three into a 0-14pt score.

The sell suitability score (0-100pt) recalculates the same set of factors in reverse, designed to internally offset and check the buy score. The gap and cross-direction between the two scores determines the strength of the final entry/exit signal.
