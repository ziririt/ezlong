What is Swing Signal Dashboard?

The Swing Signal Dashboard is a free, daily-refreshing tool that consolidates the most important US stock market sentiment indicators into a single, clean view. Instead of checking five different websites every morning, you get one unified dashboard that tells you whether the current market environment is favorable for buying, holding, or exercising caution.

The dashboard tracks four proven market-timing signals: the ATMR (Average True Market Range) indicator, the CBOE VIX volatility index, the equity put/call ratio, and the CNN Fear & Greed Index. Each of these signals captures a different dimension of market psychology — and when they align, the signal is especially powerful.

This tool is designed for long-term US stock investors, not day traders. It helps you make more confident, data-driven decisions about when to deploy capital and when to wait, without being sucked into daily market noise.

Why Use This Tool?

Most individual investors make buy and sell decisions based on gut feeling, news headlines, or social media sentiment — all of which are poor predictors of short-term market direction. The Swing Signal Dashboard gives you an objective, quantitative read on market conditions every single trading day.

The four indicators used are well-researched in academic finance. VIX above 30 has historically coincided with long-term buying opportunities. High put/call ratios signal excessive pessimism — often a contrarian buy signal. Extreme Fear readings on the CNN index have preceded some of the strongest market recoveries in history. Combining all four gives you a statistically more reliable signal than any single indicator alone.

How to use the Swing Signal Dashboard

  1. 1
    Open the dashboard

    Click Open Tool below. The page loads with the latest data from that trading day — no login or setup required.

  2. 2
    Read the ATMR signal

    The ATMR (Average True Market Range) indicator shows whether the market is in a high-volatility or low-volatility regime. A green signal suggests conditions are stable enough for long-term buying. A red or yellow signal suggests elevated risk.

  3. 3
    Check the VIX level

    The VIX fear index measures expected market volatility over the next 30 days. Below 20 = calm market. 20-30 = elevated anxiety. Above 30 = fear — historically a long-term buying opportunity for patient investors.

  4. 4
    Review the put/call ratio

    A high put/call ratio means investors are buying more protective puts than calls — a sign of excessive fear. Ratios above 1.0 have historically preceded market recoveries. A very low ratio signals overconfidence.

  5. 5
    Check the Fear and Greed Index

    CNN's Fear and Greed Index aggregates seven market indicators into a single 0-100 score. Below 25 = Extreme Fear (potential buying zone). Above 75 = Extreme Greed (potential caution zone).

  6. 6
    Make your decision

    Use the combined signal picture to inform — not dictate — your investment decisions. When multiple indicators simultaneously show Extreme Fear, history suggests this is a statistically strong long-term entry point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Swing Signal Dashboard?

It is a free daily dashboard that shows aggregated US stock market swing indicators — including ATMR, VIX, put/call ratio, and CNN Fear and Greed Index — so long-term investors can assess market conditions at a glance without checking multiple sources.

How often is the data updated?

The dashboard updates every US trading day (Monday through Friday, excluding US market holidays) with the latest available market data.

What is the ATMR indicator?

ATMR stands for Average True Market Range — a volatility-adjusted indicator that measures whether the current market environment is favorable for accumulating US stocks. A green ATMR signal historically has correlated with stronger 12-month forward returns.

What VIX level signals a buying opportunity?

Historically, a VIX reading above 30 has coincided with long-term buying opportunities in the US stock market. The highest fear readings — VIX above 40 — have often marked near-term market bottoms. However, VIX alone should not be your only signal.

What does the put/call ratio tell me?

The equity put/call ratio compares the volume of put options (bearish bets) to call options (bullish bets). A ratio above 1.0 indicates more investors are buying protection — a contrarian signal that the market may be overly pessimistic. Low ratios (below 0.6) suggest excessive optimism.

What is the CNN Fear and Greed Index?

The CNN Fear and Greed Index aggregates seven market indicators — including market momentum, safe haven demand, junk bond demand, and market volatility — into a single 0-to-100 score. Below 25 is Extreme Fear; above 75 is Extreme Greed.

Is this a day trading tool?

No. This dashboard is designed for long-term US stock investors who want macro-level swing signals to guide portfolio decisions over weeks and months — not for intraday trading. Day trading using these signals is not recommended.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. The Swing Signal Dashboard is completely free and requires no sign-up, no account, and no personal information of any kind.
