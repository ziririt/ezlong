What is the Investor Personality Quiz?

Choosing a stock portfolio allocation isn't just a math problem: it's also a behavioral one. Two investors with identical incomes can need very different portfolios depending on how they actually react to volatility, how long their time horizon really is, and what kind of decision-making style they have under stress. The Investor Personality Quiz captures this by asking about your age range, risk tolerance, and personality-style preferences, in a spirit similar to MBTI-style categorization.

Based on your answers, you're matched to one of 40 distinct portfolio archetypes, each with a suggested allocation across widely-used ETFs like QQQ (growth-oriented Nasdaq exposure), SPY (broad market exposure), and SCHD (dividend-focused stability). The result also explains the reasoning behind the match, not just the raw numbers.

Why Use This Tool?

Many investors pick an allocation based on what's trending or what a friend recommended, then panic-sell during the first serious downturn because the portfolio never actually matched their real risk tolerance. This quiz is designed to surface that mismatch before it costs you money, by starting from your behavioral tendencies rather than just your stated goals.

It's also simply a fun, fast way to think concretely about your own investing style: the kind of self-reflection exercise that's easy to skip but genuinely useful before committing real capital.
