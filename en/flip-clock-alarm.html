Music that starts at a whisper and wakes you slowly

Long Time, Easy Life is a bedside flip clock and a gentle wake-up alarm. Instead of a jarring electronic beep, one track from a library of more than 600 begins almost below hearing and grows slowly. It was built for mornings you drift out of, not mornings you are startled out of.

What the app does

A volume that begins as a whisper

The first sound is quiet enough that you have to listen for it. It climbs in small steps over three minutes, and if you still haven't stirred it escalates in stages. Pick up the phone and the volume drops immediately.

Over 600 wake-up tracks

From quiet piano to something that opens the day. Choose the track your alarm plays, so the same sound never wears thin.

Bedtime mode

Tap Start Bedtime and the screen dims over ninety seconds while your wake time appears on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. At night the app shows quotes chosen for sleep.

Rings through Silent and Focus

On iOS the alarm uses the system alarm layer, so it sounds at the set time even with the silent switch on, during a Focus mode, or while another app is running.

Flip clock and weather

A flip clock whose sky changes with the hour, showing current conditions and the forecast alongside. It earns its place on a nightstand.

One-time alarms for naps

Choose no weekdays and the alarm rings once. Naps and short rests need no commitment.

Three steps

  1. Set the time

    Pick a time, and pick weekdays if you want it to repeat. Leave them unselected and it rings once.

  2. Choose the music

    Pick the track you want to wake to, and set the snooze interval.

  3. Tap Start Bedtime before sleeping

    The screen dims and your wake time appears on the Lock Screen. Put the phone down and sleep.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The wake alarm, flip clock and weather are all free, and no account is required.

Will the alarm ring in silent mode?

Yes. On iOS it uses the system alarm layer, which passes through the silent switch and Focus modes. On Android it rings on the alarm channel.

Does the music play with the screen off?

It does. Tapping Start Bedtime keeps the app awake until morning, so the music plays at the set time even with the screen off.

What if I fall asleep watching YouTube or Netflix?

At your wake time the app takes over the audio and your wake music plays. Before that it leaves whatever you are watching alone.

Does the volume jump suddenly?

No. The first three minutes climb in small steps from a near-silent start. If you still don't wake it escalates, but the steps are spaced so nothing lurches.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes, it runs in a browser. Try it on the web first and install the app if you like it.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish and Portuguese, chosen automatically to match your device.

Can I set a one-time alarm for a nap?

Yes. In Add new alarm, leave every weekday unselected and the alarm rings once.

The alarm isn't appearing on screen — why?

Some Android phones suppress the alarm screen when full-screen notifications or background pop-up permission are switched off. The app's alarm settings screen diagnoses this and takes you straight to the right setting.
