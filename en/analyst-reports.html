What is Wall Street Analyst Price Targets?

Professional Wall Street analysts publish 12-month price targets for the stocks they cover, based on detailed financial modeling. This tool aggregates those targets for 43 major US stocks — including TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, and MSFT — and shows you the average target, the implied upside or downside from the current price, and the full range of opinion from Bull case to Bear case.

Alongside price targets, you'll also see the breakdown of Buy, Hold, and Sell ratings from covering analysts, so you can gauge not just the price expectation but how much consensus actually exists behind it.

Why Use This Tool?

A single analyst's price target can be noisy — but looking at the average across dozens of analysts, along with the full Bull-to-Bear range, gives you a much more balanced read on professional sentiment toward a stock. It's a useful sanity check against your own valuation assumptions.

This is especially useful before making a large position decision: if your own thesis is far outside the professional range in either direction, that's worth investigating further before you act.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Wall Street price target?

A price target is a professional stock analyst's estimate of where a stock's price will be, typically over the next 12 months, based on their financial models and research.

Which stocks are covered?

43 major US stocks are covered, including TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, and META, spanning technology, consumer, and other large-cap sectors.

What do Bull and Bear scenarios mean?

The Bull scenario shows the single highest price target among covering analysts, while the Bear scenario shows the lowest — giving you the full range of professional opinion, not just the average.

How reliable are analyst price targets?

Analyst price targets are informed estimates, not guarantees, and are frequently revised as new information emerges. They should be treated as one data point among many, not a certainty.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Wall Street Analyst Price Targets is completely free and requires no sign-up, no account, and no personal information of any kind.
