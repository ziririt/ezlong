What is Simple Stock Prices?

Simple Stock Prices is a free, no-clutter live price tracker covering the top 100 US stocks by market capitalization — names like NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT, and AMZN — refreshed every 15 minutes throughout the trading day. Each stock is shown with its current price, percentage change, and a compact sparkline chart so you can see the recent trend at a glance.

Alongside individual stocks, the tool tracks the S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, and Dow Jones Industrial Average, giving you the broader market context in the same view. A built-in portfolio feature lets you save the specific tickers you follow without creating an account.

Why Use This Tool?

Most free stock price sites are cluttered with ads, pop-ups, and unrelated news. Simple Stock Prices strips all of that away and shows exactly what long-term investors actually need: price, change, trend, and index context — nothing more, nothing less.

Because it's organized by market cap rank, it also doubles as a quick way to see which of the world's largest companies are moving the most on any given day, without needing to know every ticker symbol in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are prices updated?

Stock prices are refreshed every 15 minutes during US market hours, including pre-market and after-hours sessions where available.

Which stocks are covered?

The top 100 US stocks by market capitalization are covered, including NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, and META, along with major sector ETFs.

Does it show index levels too?

Yes. The S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, and Dow Jones Industrial Average are all tracked alongside individual stock prices.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Simple Stock Prices is completely free and requires no sign-up, no account, and no personal information of any kind.
