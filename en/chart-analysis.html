What is AI Technical Chart Analysis?

Reading a stock chart properly means tracking multiple technical indicators at once — MACD for momentum, RSI for overbought or oversold conditions, and Bollinger Bands for volatility and mean reversion. Most retail investors either skip this entirely or misread the signals. AI Technical Chart Analysis has AI do this reading for you, across 50 tickers including TSLA, NVDA, QQQ, select Korean stocks, and major cryptocurrencies.

For each ticker, the AI produces a plain-language summary of what the current indicator readings suggest, alongside the interactive chart itself so you can verify the underlying MACD, RSI, and Bollinger Band lines directly. This gives you both the AI's interpretation and the raw data behind it — all in the tool above, no separate app or sign-up required.

Why Use This Tool?

Technical indicators are only useful if you read them consistently and correctly — and doing that manually for dozens of tickers, every day, isn't realistic for most individual investors. This tool automates that reading process so you can quickly scan across many tickers and drill into the ones that show an interesting setup.

Because the analysis covers US stocks, Korean stocks, and crypto in one place, it's also useful for investors who hold a mixed portfolio across markets and want a single, consistent technical read across all of them.
